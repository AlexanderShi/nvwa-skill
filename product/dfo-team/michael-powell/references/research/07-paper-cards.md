## What the cards are

Each card is one work read against the D1–D8 dimensions of `references/paper-reading-card.md` (problem entry, core idea, assumptions, proof devices, experiments, positioning, writing, contribution and limits), with page references taken from the `[[page N]]` markers of the extracted text, a **Method links** block (evidence ✅ / variant ⚠ / contradiction ✗ against the Core Research Methods and other sections of `SKILL.md`), new-pattern candidates and transferable techniques. Cards live in batch files under `cards/`: `c01`–`c06` core full cards for 1997–2015 and the Royal Society memoir; `c2-01`, `c2-02` core full cards for the classic papers of 1963–1990; `s01`, `s02`, `s2-01` supplement short cards (RBF papers, the 2009 retrospective, the 2014 SAO paper, the duplicate S186); `a2-01`–`a2-05` abstract- and metadata-level cards, one batch per period. There is no separate book batch: the books and edited volumes (S009, S199, S119, S133, D002) have no open full text and are carded at abstract or metadata level inside the `a2` batches. Every batch file opens with its batch intent (goal, focus dimensions, material roles). Each `*.digest.json` is the machine-readable summary of its batch file, and this index is built from those digests.

Most full texts are DAMTP report versions of the published papers, so page references are report pages (each card's header names the version read). The extracted texts are git-ignored; `references/sources/papers/INDEX.md` lists what was downloaded.
