# Paper cards: index of the deep reading

> Charles Audet · research-craft skill · deep-reading stage · generated 2026-09-27 from `cards/*.digest.json` (one digest record per card).

## What the cards are

Each work on Audet's publication list was read at the deepest level its available text allowed and written up as a card in the format of `references/paper-reading-card.md` (dimensions D1–D8, method links, transferable techniques, page-cited quotes). Cards are grouped in batch files under `cards/`: `c*` = core full cards, `s*` = supplement short cards, `b*` = book-length works (theses), `a*` = abstract-level and metadata-level cards. Every card has a machine-readable twin `cards/<batch>.digest.json`. The synthesis of all cards into method evidence, variants, promotions and heuristics is in `08-deep-reading-synthesis.md`; SKILL.md is updated from that file, not from this index.

Page references in the cards use the `[[page N]]` markers of the extracted text files (PDF page numbers), and the version read is named on each card (often the arXiv or GERAD preprint rather than the journal version).

## Legend

- **Read level**: `full` (full text read), `partial` (read in part), `unreadable` (text file not the named work or garbled), `abstract` (abstract only), `metadata` (title and venue only).
- **Methods linked**: links recorded on the card to SKILL.md elements, with the relation: `e` = practice evidence, `v` = variant, `c` = contradiction. `M1`–`M6` = Core Research Methods 1–6; `H7`–`H10` = Research Heuristics; `Taste`, `Anatomy` (Signature Work Anatomy), `Trajectory`, `Lineage`, `Tensions`, `Roundtable`, `WfB`/`WfC` (Workflows). New-pattern candidates are not listed here; they are clustered in `08-deep-reading-synthesis.md`.
- **One-line contribution**: the first sentence of the card's contribution field (shortened where needed).
