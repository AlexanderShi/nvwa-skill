### S043 · Mesh-based Nelder–Mead algorithm for inequality constrained optimization (Computational Optimization and Applications 2018, 10.1007/s10589-018-0016-0)

### S055 · Robust optimization of noisy blackbox problems using the mesh adaptive direct search algorithm (Optimization Letters 2018, 10.1007/s11590-017-1226-6)
**Read**: abstract. Source: Optimization Online 2016/07/5551, no PDF attached; that page cites Optimization Letters 12(4):675–689, 2018. Full text not read. · **Coauthors**: A. Ihaddadene, S. Le Digabel, C. Tribes
