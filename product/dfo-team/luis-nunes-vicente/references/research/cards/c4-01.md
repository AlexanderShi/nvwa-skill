### S121 · SIAG on Optimization Views and News — Chair's Column (SIAG/OPT Views and News 33(1) 2025, no DOI/arXiv)
**Role**: core (the column only) · **Coauthors**: none for the column. It is signed "Luis Nunes Vicente (Chair, SIAG on Optimization)" with the same Lehigh titles as in 2023 (p. 14). Scholar lists J. Diakonikolas, D. Drusvyatskiy, M. Menickelly and P. Belotti. They are the lead author and the editors of other items in the issue (contents, p. 1). · **Position in trajectory**: the closing report of his 2023–2025 term. It covers "The past three years" (p. 14) and ends by wishing "the new elected officers" a successful "2026–2028 term" (p. 14). **Text read**: the column in full, p. 14 (file lines 1098–1174). The book announcement above it (p. 13) and the editors' comments below it (p. 15) were read only to find where the column starts and ends.

- **D1 Problem & entry**: An end-of-term report. It opens with a one-sentence verdict: the three years were "a period of continued progress and consolidation" for SIAG/OPT (p. 14). It then gives a field-level framing, the only research-facing sentence in either column. The field evolves through "scalable stochastic methods", mixed-integer nonlinear programming, and "early progress at the interface of optimization and quantum computing". These are said to "reinforce optimization’s foundational role in scientific computing, data science, and machine learning" (p. 14).
- **D2 Core idea (the agenda)**: The report is organized under five run-in headings, each saying what the board did (p. 14):
  1. *Membership*: SIAG/OPT "remains one of SIAM’s largest and most active activity groups". Membership fell after the pandemic, rebounded around SIOPT 2023, and further growth is expected in 2026. The group keeps links with the SIAGs on CSE, Data Science, ACDA, Imaging Science and Mathematics of Planet Earth.
  2. *Sponsored Events*: the SIAG co-sponsored MOPTA at Lehigh each year from 2023 to 2025, with "roughly 140 participants annually". MOPTA's value is said to lie "particularly" in being "not affiliated with other professional societies". The 2023 Gene Golub SIAM Summer School, also at Lehigh, "emphasized optimization with a focus on quantum computing" for early-career researchers.
  3. *Prizes*: the term's "major accomplishment" was a permanent endowment for the Test of Time Award, raised through "a $25,000 fundraising effort". The 2026 prizes drew 23 nominations (Best Paper), 14 (Early Career) and 20 (Test of Time).
  4. *2026 SIAM Conference on Optimization*: after "an open international call", the University of Edinburgh was selected (June 2–5, 2026). The plenary lineup was chosen with "strong attention to inclusiveness across research areas and origins of speakers". The column describes the venue and expects "close to 1,500 talks".
  5. *Acknowledgements*: the "core activities" are listed as the triennial conference, the prizes, the newsletter and the SIAM Journal on Optimization, and the website and newsletter were kept "up to date".

  The open call closes the loop that S122 opened (S122 pp. 23–24; S121 p. 14).
- **D3 Assumptions & model**: n/a (service column; no mathematical model). The membership trend is stated without figures (p. 14).
- **D4 Analysis**: n/a (no proofs). The quantitative content is counts only: $25,000, the nomination numbers 23/14/20, roughly 140 MOPTA participants a year, and close to 1,500 talks (p. 14). No membership numbers are given for the claimed dip and rebound (p. 14).
- **D5 Experiments**: none.
- **D6 Positioning**: SIAG/OPT is placed among neighbouring SIAGs, not against them (p. 14). Research priorities are named only at field level. Stochastic methods come first, followed by MINLP and quantum computing, and the list ends with data science and machine learning (p. 14). The column does not mention derivative-free optimization or his own research (p. 14). Credit goes to the fellow officers (Cartis, Eichfelder, Mueller) and to the leaders of SIOPT 2026 (Anjos, Eichfelder, Schewe), with the committees and minisymposium organizers (p. 14). Both sponsored events it names, MOPTA and the 2023 Gene Golub Summer School, took place at Lehigh (p. 14), where he holds the ISE department chair (signature, p. 14). The column states this location but draws no conclusion from it.
- **D7 Writing & rhetoric**:
  - *Format*: a report with run-in section headings (p. 14), unlike the letter-like 2023 column (S122 pp. 23–24).
  - *Voice*: "we" (the board) throughout. "I" appears only in the thanks ("I would like to thank my fellow officers", p. 14).
  - *Claims*: backed by counts where counts exist (p. 14). The enthusiasm markers go with the counts: "This is a truly phenomenal response" after the nomination numbers, and "We are getting close to 1,500 talks!" (p. 14).
  - *Register*: warm but less personal than 2023. There are no first-name addresses except "it was a great pleasure working with you!" (p. 14). No hedging beyond "we anticipate" for membership growth (p. 14).
- **D8 Contribution & limits**: Service contributions: the endowment (p. 14), three years of MOPTA co-sponsorship (p. 14), site selection for 2026 by open call (p. 14), and upkeep of the website and newsletter (p. 14). No limitation is stated. The on-line mini-courses solicited in 2023 (S122 p. 24) are not mentioned in this report (p. 14), so their outcome is not documented here. Future items: the awards to be celebrated at SIOPT 2026 in Edinburgh and the new officers' 2026–2028 term (p. 14).
