## What the cards are

Each card is one paper read against the D1–D8 dimensions of `references/paper-reading-card.md` (problem entry, core idea, assumptions, proof devices, experiments, positioning, writing, contribution and limits), with page references taken from the `[[page N]]` markers of the extracted text, a **Method links** block (evidence ✅ / variant ⚠ / contradiction ✗ against the Core Research Methods of `SKILL.md`), new-pattern candidates and transferable techniques. Cards live in batch files under `cards/`: `c*` core full cards, `s*` supplement short cards, `c3-*` the Optima 79 essay, `a*` abstract- and metadata-level cards. Every batch file opens with its batch intent (goal, focus dimensions, material roles). Each `*.digest.json` is the machine-readable summary of its batch file, and this index is built from those digests. There is no separate book batch: the one book (S001, the 2009 IDFO textbook) has no open full text and is carded at abstract level in `a2-01`.
