## 2. Patterns (inference from the table — secondary, my analysis)

- **Juniors first-author the stochastic-oracle papers.** In 2014–2026, the first author is nearly always a junior co-author; Scheinberg is typically last or second-to-last. This is consistent with a group model where a student/postdoc owns one oracle setting and one proof.
- **Continuity of lines across generations.** Menickelly (2018, 2019) → Paquette (2020) → Cao/Berahas (2022, 2024) → Jin/Xie (2024) → Chaudhry/Sun (2025–26): each generation inherits the probabilistic-oracle framework and pushes one step (expected → high-probability → corrupted/heavy-tailed; line search → trust region → Powell-style DFO). *Inference.*
- **Pairing a junior with an outside specialist.** Probability (Blanchet), complexity (Cartis), ML (Choromanski) specialists appear on papers with juniors. *Inference.*
- **Code lives with the junior co-author** (e.g., Cao's DFOTR repo). *Inference from one repo.*
