> Date: 2026-09-27. Generated from `cards/*.digest.json` by a one-off script that is not shipped; when a card is added or changed, write or update its row by hand from the digest fields, in the format of the other rows. The synthesis of these cards is `08-deep-reading-synthesis.md`.

## What the cards are

Each card is one paper read against the D1–D8 dimensions of `references/paper-reading-card.md` (problem entry, core idea, assumptions, proof devices, experiments, positioning, writing, contribution and limits), with page references taken from the `[[page N]]` markers of the extracted text, a **Method links** block (evidence ✅ / variant ⚠ / contradiction ✗ against the Core Research Methods of `SKILL.md`), new-pattern candidates and transferable techniques. Cards live in batch files under `cards/`: `c*` core full cards, `s*` supplement short cards, `c3-01` Conn's first-person essay (S236), `s4-01` the OCR re-read of S049, and `a*` abstract- and metadata-level cards. There is no separate book batch: the two books with Conn as author (S001 *Trust-Region Methods*, S002 *Introduction to Derivative-Free Optimization*) and the LANCELOT book (S006) have no open full text and are carded at abstract level in `a2-02`. Every batch file opens with its batch intent (goal, focus dimensions, material roles). Each `*.digest.json` is the machine-readable summary of its batch file, and this index is built from those digests.

Scanned early papers (S018, S021, S030, S046, S048, S108, S130, S164) were read from page images; their cards carry page references but no verbatim quotes. OCR text for them now exists in `references/sources/papers/txt/` (git-ignored) for spot checks.
