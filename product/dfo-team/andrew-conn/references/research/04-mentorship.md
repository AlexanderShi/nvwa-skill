**Practice evidence of the student → co-author pattern:** Coleman & Conn, "Nonlinear programming via an exact penalty function: Global analysis" and "…Asymptotic analysis", Math. Programming 24 (1982) (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01585101). The thesis topic was turned into a paired theory publication: global analysis plus local/asymptotic analysis.

**Topic continuity:** Li's minimax thesis (1988) sits on Conn's nonsmooth/penalty line. Minimax is also what the IBM EE colleague later asked Conn about, which opened the circuit-tuning work (MAM 2015 profile). This connection is an observation, not a claim made by any source.

## Colleagues' characterisations (others' observations)

- "Known to everyone as Andy". Played "a pioneering role in nonlinear optimization and remained very active in the field until shortly before his death" (SIAM obituary snippet, wording shown consistently).
- His move to IBM in 1990 was made "with the encouragement of Ellis L. Johnson" (paraphrase; Wikipedia "Ellis L. Johnson" snippet — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellis_L._Johnson). Secondary.
- Self-image as relayed in the 2015 profile: he was seen, and was recommended, as an approachable mathematician for engineers (paraphrase; https://ww2.amstat.org/mam/2015/highlighted/MAM2015profile_Conn.pdf).
