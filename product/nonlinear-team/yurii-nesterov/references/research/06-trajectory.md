## 0. The trajectory in brief

1. **Four settings, four funding frames.**
   - Soviet Academy institute, 1977–1992.
   - CORE Louvain, 1993–2021/23, with the ERC Advanced Grant 2018–2024 at the end.
   - Corvinus Budapest, with a Hungarian grant, from 2023/24.
   - Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen and SLAI), fractional from 2024 and full-time from June 2026.

   Each move followed the end of a frame: the USSR's dissolution, then retirement and the end of the ERC grant. Each move also brought a new partner group [practice, P; the pattern is inferred].
2. **Research lines are not dropped. They are parked and re-entered decades later, each time with a new tool.** [practice, P]
   - Fast gradient: 1983 → smoothing and composite (2003–07) → universal (2013, 2025).
   - IPMs: 1988–2002 → parabolic target space (2008) → non-symmetric and hyperbolic cones (2012, 2016) → the Budapest programme (2023–26).
   - Second order: 1984 thesis on degenerate problems → cubic regularization (2006) → tensor, quasi-Newton rates and super-universal Newton (2018–24) → high-order global curvature bounds (2025–26).
   - Nonsmooth differentiation: 1987–88 → *Math. Program.* 2005.
3. **He explains his own turns by changes outside mathematics.** [stated, P] Computers and the Internet made models bigger, so IPMs became too expensive per iteration (2013 dissertation). Big web problems pushed him back to coordinate methods (2010). He needed a strong IPM group to work with (2024). AI "gives us many interesting research directions" (2024) and "many new challenges" (2026). He never explains a turn by a failed result.
4. **Timing.**
   - He entered his two most-cited lines **ahead of the consensus**: fast gradient (1983; widely used only from the mid-2000s) and conic IPMs via self-concordance (1988–94; SDP "exploded" afterwards).
   - He entered high-order methods **after others** had started (2018), with a twist of his own: the convex regularized model.
   - He re-entered IPMs in 2023–26 **against the trend**. IPM attention had "turned away" (Corvinus 2024, the journalist's paraphrase).
   - Details in §3 and §4.
5. **Last 12 months (Sep 2025 – Sep 2026)** [practice, P]. The output has three strands:
   - IPM theory with the Budapest group: SIOPT 2026; arXiv 2603.21500 and 2605.12658.
   - Universal and high-order complexity: arXiv 2509.20902 and 2511.07341 (with Doikov).
   - Editorial work: the Polyak memorial issue, JOTA 2025.

   Honours: the Gauss Prize (23 July 2026) and, per CUHK-Shenzhen, the NAAI Academy Award (2026). He moved full-time to Shenzhen in June 2026. He gives AI as his motivation, but no AI or ML paper appears in this window. See §8.
