# Notes: "It's to Solve Problems – An Interview with Roger Fletcher" (Optima 99, December 2015)

- Source: Sven Leyffer (interviewer), *Optima* 99 (Mathematical Optimization Society Newsletter), December 2015, pp. 1–6; transcription of a recorded conversation by Gail Pieper (credit line on p. 5).
- URL: https://www.mathopt.org/optima/99/optima_99.pdf (fetched 2026-09-28 with curl; issue metadata from https://www.mathopt.org/api/Optima/ByNumber/99?includeArticles=true).
- Same issue: Philippe Toint, "Impressions of Roger's Interview" (p. 6); Frank E. Curtis, "Young Researchers Would Be Wise to Read this Interview!" (pp. 6–7).
- Type: primary, stated (Fletcher's own words, spoken, aged about 76). Toint and Curtis pieces: secondary, observed.
- Copyright: MOS newsletter. This file keeps only short excerpts for research notes; the full PDF stays local (not committed). Excerpts are verbatim except that PDF-extraction artifacts (split words such as "Y ou", ligatures) were repaired.
